Frequently asked questions

Answers before you commit

What files are accepted?

Common working formats include CSV, TSV, Excel, PDF, common image formats, text documents, and copied local file sets. Send one redacted sample first. The written quote confirms the exact format and batch.

How long does work take?

Each service includes a typical planning range. The written quote confirms the actual range after source quality, volume, edge cases, current availability, and review requirements are known. The range is an estimate, not a guarantee.

How is confidentiality handled?

Initial review should use a public URL or redacted sample. If private material is required later, the written scope identifies the approved working copy, handling boundary, permitted access, and any retention or deletion requirement before transfer.

Are revisions included?

The written quote identifies the review point and revision allowance. Corrections to the agreed deliverable are separated from new requirements. Material additions receive a new fixed-scope confirmation before work continues.

When is payment requested?

Payment timing is stated in the written quote. For small fixed-scope engagements, payment is normally requested after the agreed delivery is made available for review unless the quote states a different milestone.

Can everything be handled in writing?

Yes. Email is the default path for requests, scope, price, approvals, questions, changes, blocked items, and final handoff. Written communication is fully supported for people who prefer or need to avoid calls.

Does SPARTAN provide legal or accounting advice?

No. SPARTAN can organize provided information and prepare administrative files, but does not interpret law, provide legal advice, provide accounting advice, certify compliance, or make regulated professional decisions.

How are larger projects scoped?

Larger or mixed-service work is divided into fixed milestones. Each milestone identifies its inputs, deliverables, price, planning range, review point, and exclusions before approval.
